How to Play

Click a tile, then click its matching pair to remove both.

Two tiles match if they are the same type. With Chinese style enabled (default), any flower matches any flower and any season matches any season.

Tiles can be connected if a straight line passes through empty space with at most two 90° turns (three straight segments). If "Tiles can slide" is enabled, sliding allows tiles to move into empty space so two lines (one corner) may be enough.

Right-click (or long press) to highlight matching tiles.

Use Hint to find a valid move. Use Undo/Redo to backtrack.
