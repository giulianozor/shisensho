How to Play

The goal is to remove every tile of the board in pairs. Click a tile, then its partner: both disappear and the tiles above them fall down if Gravity is on.

Two tiles match when they are of the same kind. With Chinese style on, which is the default, any flower matches any flower and any season matches any season.

Two matching tiles can only be removed when they are connected by a line that does not cross any other tile. The line may turn twice, so it has three straight parts. Right click a tile to mark it: all tiles of the same kind are then drawn marked, which makes the partner easy to find. On a touch screen, hold the tile instead.

With Tiles can slide out of the way on, a tile standing in the way may slide into the empty space at the end of its row or column, and two matching tiles then only need a single corner to be connected. The line that needs a slide is drawn dashed: the dashes are the part of the way that has to be freed first.

If more than one way is possible, the game draws a line for each of them: click on the one you prefer. The Hint button marks a pair that can be removed right now.

Undo and Redo step through the moves, Pause stops the clock. A game that ends without a single Undo or Hint is worth a place in the high scores, which are kept in the browser.
